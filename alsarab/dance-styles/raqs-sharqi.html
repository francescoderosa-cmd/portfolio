About

Raqs Sharqi at Al Sarab

Raqs Sharqi is taught here as a technique with a syllabus, not as a party skill. Classes work through isolations, layering, travelling steps and — the part most schools skip — rhythm reading, so a dancer can name what she is hearing before she dances to it.

The genre is directed by Dr. Nadra Assaf, who teaches the upper levels herself. Adult classes are the most common entry point into the school.

LevelsBeginner, Intermediate, Open

What to expect

  • Hip scarf provided for the first class
  • Rhythm reading as part of the syllabus
  • Classical Egyptian repertoire in the upper levels

Dr. Nadra Assaf

Dr. Nadra Majeed Assaf founded Al Sarab Alternative Dance School and Al Sarab Dance Company in January 1991. For more than thirty years she has built the school around embodied pedagogy — dance taught as an academic discipline — in Lebanon and across the Middle East.

السرابAL SARAB

Al Sarab Alternative Dance School is a curriculum-based dance school at Center Al Haref in Byblos, Lebanon. A place where technique, discipline and belonging meet. We teach Modern, Contemporary, Classical Ballet, Jazz, Raqs Sharqi and Acro, from creative movement at three years old to pre-professional levels. Registered with the Lebanese Ministry of Education under licence 213/2009 and a member of the National Dance Education Organization (USA). We also open our studios for rehearsals, workshops and private group sessions.
