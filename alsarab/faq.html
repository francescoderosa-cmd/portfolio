Classes & Schedule

How the week runs, and what a term looks like.

01How do I register for a course?+
Call +961 71 049 801 or send us a message. We book a trial class in the level we think fits, and confirm the year place after it.
02Can I try a class before committing?+
Yes. Every new student is booked into a trial class first — it is how we place people. During Open Studios Week in September, any class can be tried at no cost.
03How long is a class?+
Creative movement is 40 minutes. Every other class is 55 minutes.
04Which genres do you teach?+
Modern, Contemporary, Classical Ballet, Jazz, Raqs Sharqi and Acro — taught inside one written curriculum.
05How long is a term?+
The academic year runs from late September to the end of June, following the school calendar.

Getting Started

For anyone who has never taken a dance class.

01Do I need any dance experience?+
No. Beginner and Open levels start from zero at every age, including adults.
02What should I wear?+
Comfortable clothes you can move in, hair tied back. Ballet needs ballet shoes; most other genres are barefoot.
03What do I need to bring?+
Water and the clothes you will dance in. A hip scarf is provided for a first Raqs Sharqi class.
04How do you decide which level my child joins?+
By age and by level, after a trial class. Age alone does not place a student.
05Is there an age limit for adults?+
No. Adult Open classes run in Modern, Ballet, Jazz and Raqs Sharqi.
06Which genre should I start with?+
See our beginner's guide, or call us and describe what you are after.

Other

Everything else.

01How do I apply to teach at Al Sarab?+
Write to alsarab.dance@gmail.com with your training and teaching experience.
02What makes Al Sarab different from other schools?+
It is curriculum-based: written level objectives, an assessment officer and an end-of-year evaluation, under Ministry of Education licence 213/2009 and NDEO membership.
03Is Al Sarab Dance Company the same as the school?+
They share a founder and a home but are separate. The school teaches; the company performs and tours.

Contact the school
السرابAL SARAB

Al Sarab Alternative Dance School is a curriculum-based dance school at Center Al Haref in Byblos, Lebanon. A place where technique, discipline and belonging meet. We teach Modern, Contemporary, Classical Ballet, Jazz, Raqs Sharqi and Acro, from creative movement at three years old to pre-professional levels. Registered with the Lebanese Ministry of Education under licence 213/2009 and a member of the National Dance Education Organization (USA). We also open our studios for rehearsals, workshops and private group sessions.
