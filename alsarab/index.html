About the school

Thirty-five years of dance education in Lebanon

For over 30 years, Al Sarab Alternative Dance School has been a place committed to the embodied pedagogy of the dancers of Lebanon. The school opened in January 1991 with a focus on Modern Dance; over the past fifteen years it has grown into a curriculum covering six movement disciplines.

Al Sarab is a curriculum-based school registered with the Ministry of Education (Lebanon) under licence number 213/2009, and a member of the National Dance Education Organization (USA) — one of the very few schools in the country to hold both.

Our mission is to open opportunities in the society for any and every body to dance.Dr. Nadra Majeed Assaf, Founding Director

The school's home is in Jbeil (Byblos), with branches that have carried the programme north to Koura and south towards Beirut. Its sister ensemble, Al Sarab Dance Company, tours separately — you can find them at alsarabdancecompany.com.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything families ask us before the first class — registration, ages, levels and what to bring.

01Where is Al Sarab Dance School?+
The main studio is at Center Al Haref, Byblos (Jbeil). The school also runs branches in Bishmizzine, Koura, La Collina and Rabieh. Reception is open Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 4PM to 9PM.
02Does my child need dance experience to start?+
No. Beginners start from zero at every age. Placement is by age and by level: creative movement from 3 years, foundation classes from 6, and technique levels from 9 upwards. New adult beginners are welcome in Raqs Sharqi and Modern.
03Which dance genres do you teach?+
Jazz, Modern, Classical Ballet, Raqs Sharqi, Acro and Contemporary — taught inside one written curriculum, so a student moves between genres without repeating the same year of training twice.
04How do I register?+
Send us a message or call +961 71 049 801. We book a trial class in the level we think fits, and confirm the year place after it. Fees and the full timetable are given at registration.
05What does “curriculum-based” actually mean?+
Every level has written objectives, an assessment officer, and an end-of-year evaluation. Progress is recorded, not assumed. It is what our Ministry of Education licence and our NDEO membership are built on.
06Is Al Sarab Dance Company the same as the school?+
They share a founder and a home, but they are separate. The school teaches; the company performs and tours. You can find the company at alsarabdancecompany.com.
