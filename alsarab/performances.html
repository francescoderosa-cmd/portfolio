Al Sarab at your event

Weddings, private parties, galas and commissioned shows. Al Sarab has been putting dancers on stage in Lebanon since 1991 — including a full production at Metro Al Madina in Beirut. The same dancers can perform at your event.

What this is

We come to you and perform

This is the performance side of the school: a choreographed set danced by Al Sarab at your wedding, your party or your event. It is not a class — nobody in the room has to dance.

If what you actually want is your own group learning a routine together, that is a private session instead. If you want the room, not the dancers, see studio rental.

Formats

Four ways to book us

Every booking is quoted individually — the format sets the shape, then the length, the number of dancers and the travel set the fee.

Wedding15–40 min4–10 dancers

Wedding

An entrance, a set piece, or a full sequence built into the evening. The zaffe tradition and a choreographed Raqs Sharqi number are the two most-asked-for; both can be cut to the length your venue allows.

  • Choreography built for your run of show
  • Costume and music agreed in advance
  • Site visit or floor plan check before the date
  • Travel across Lebanon
Repertoire: Raqs Sharqi, Jazz, ModernEnquire about this
Private party10–25 min3–6 dancers

Private party

A short, high-energy set for a birthday, an anniversary or a house celebration. Small enough to work in a room without a stage.

  • Works without a stage or rig
  • Repertoire from our existing pieces
  • One set or two shorter appearances
  • Music supplied or matched to your DJ

Corporate & gala

A programmed piece for an opening, an award evening or a company celebration — closer to a concert set than to entertainment.

  • Programmed as a sequence, not a single number
  • Technical rider for lighting and sound
  • Rehearsal in the venue where possible
  • Bilingual introduction if needed

A full-length commissioned work, or an existing production restaged for your venue — as Al Sarab did at Metro Al Madina in Beirut.

  • Creation period agreed with you
  • Full technical and wardrobe production
  • Photography and filming arranged

Good to know

01How far in advance should we book?+
For a wedding in high season, as early as you can — choreography, costume and rehearsal all sit before the date. For a short party set, a few weeks is usually enough. Placeholder: confirm the real lead times.
02Do you travel outside Byblos?+
Yes, across Lebanon. Travel is quoted with the booking. Placeholder: confirm whether there is a distance limit and how travel is charged.
03Can the choreography be made for us specifically?+
Yes. Every wedding and gala booking is built around your run of show, your venue and the music you want. Existing repertoire is the faster and cheaper route if you prefer it.
04What do you need from the venue?+
A flat, clean performance area and a way to play the music. Anything beyond that — lighting, a raised stage, a sound engineer — is agreed in the technical rider.
05How much does it cost?+
Rates depend on the format, the number of dancers, the length and the travel. Every booking is quoted individually — send an enquiry and we will come back within 1 to 2 working days.
06Is this the same thing as booking a class?+
No. Here Al Sarab comes to you and performs. If you want your group to learn a dance themselves, that is a private session instead.

السرابAL SARAB

Al Sarab Alternative Dance School is a curriculum-based dance school at Center Al Haref in Byblos, Lebanon. A place where technique, discipline and belonging meet. We teach Modern, Contemporary, Classical Ballet, Jazz, Raqs Sharqi and Acro, from creative movement at three years old to pre-professional levels. Registered with the Lebanese Ministry of Education under licence 213/2009 and a member of the National Dance Education Organization (USA). We also open our studios for rehearsals, workshops and private group sessions.
